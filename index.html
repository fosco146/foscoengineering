Bottlenecks I’ve Cleared

Closed jobs that never got billed

A national technical services firm

Problem
Jobs got stuck between the field and the invoice, and nobody could see where. Some were finished but never fully billed; others sat half-done waiting on someone. There was no single place to see what was stuck, or whose move it was.
Fix
I turned the job-tracking software the firm already paid for into a live board showing every job's stage and owner, then added automatic email reminders when a job stalled. Every job had a clear owner, everyone knew what was on their plate, and nothing sat quietly.
Result
$250K+ in unbilled work recovered, and a shorter cycle from job close to invoice.

Two hours back on every job closeout

Same firm, 100+ job closeouts a year

Problem
Every job closeout ate hours of office time: re-typing techs' field data into Word, formatting tables, hunting through folders and assembling the closeout package by hand. Then someone checked it all for errors.
Fix

Common questions

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Mostly HVAC, mechanical and roofing contractors with about 10–100 people. If your team runs jobs and bills when they're done, the bottlenecks are usually the same.

Do I have to buy new software?

Usually not. I start with the tools you already have, like your spreadsheets, accounting software or job management system, and only recommend something new when it clearly pays for itself.

How much does it cost?

The first 30-minute call is free. Audits are then quoted at a fixed fee and are credited in full toward the build. Builds are quoted at a fixed fee after the audit, in writing, before any work starts. No hourly billing, no surprises.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm available to meet in person in Boston, MA, and everything else works remotely.

How long does it take?

The audit typically takes no more than two weeks. Most builds take between two and six weeks, depending on scope.

Where does AI fit in?

Only where it saves real time. I don't add AI for its own sake.

What if the audit doesn't find anything worth fixing?

You still get the ranked list of bottlenecks. It's yours whether we do more business together or not.

Do you need access to our books and systems? Is that safe?

I use read-only access wherever possible and sign an NDA on request. Any data I take out of your systems is deleted when the project ends.

Who owns what you build?

You do. It's built in your accounts and fully documented.

What happens after handoff?

You're never left stranded. Everything is documented so your team can run it day to day, and if you want ongoing help, I can build support into your quote.
